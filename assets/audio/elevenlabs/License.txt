ElevenLabs text-to-sound model (eleven_text_to_sound_v2), on Max's ElevenLabs account

On the free plan, ElevenLabs asks for this attribution and allows non-commercial use only:
check ElevenLabs' terms (https://elevenlabs.io/terms-of-use) before any commercial use, or
regenerate them on a paid plan.

Processed for the game: MP3 decoded, mixed to mono, 44.1 kHz, silence trimmed at both ends of
the one-shots, long ones cut with a fade out, short fades in and out, levels matched (RMS of the
sound about -16 dBFS, peak at most -1 dBFS), encoded as OGG Vorbis (quality about 3). The loops
(engine, squeal, wind, rumble: generated as loops) are only levelled: the game crossfades their
seam itself (src/audio/dsp.rs, seamless_loop).
