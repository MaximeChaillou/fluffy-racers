Used in Fluffy Racers at Max's request (issue #247, 2026-11-11: « Récupère les sons de tuxkart
ou supertuxkart qui sont libres »). Processed for the game (modified): decoded, mixed to mono,
resampled to 44.1 kHz (skid.ogg was 96 kHz), DC removed, levels matched (RMS about -16 dBFS,
peak at most -1 dBFS), short fades on crash.ogg, encoded as OGG Vorbis. The loops (engines,
skid) are not cut: the game crossfades their seam itself (src/audio/dsp.rs, seamless_loop).
